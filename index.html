For Shivika
❦
Before you begin…

If birthdays were made of stars, I would have borrowed the whole sky for you.

This little place isn’t a website.
It’s a quiet corner where I’ve hidden pieces of my heart ❤️
The moments that stayed,
The smiles that never left,
And the love that kept growing in the spaces between our days.

So pause for a moment.✋🏻
Let the world become a little quieter,🫶🏻
And walk through these pages the way we walk together 👫🏻
Slowly, gently, and hand in hand.🤝🏻

Tonight is about you.🌌
About the girl who made ordinary days feel unforgettable.

Happy Birthday, my love.
Now come a little closer…

I'm ready ❤️
Happy Birthday
A letter written only for you.
💌

Thank you for opening this little world I made for you.

The page before this was an invitation, this page is the beginning of everything I’ve been carrying quietly inside my heart.

Before the cake, before the wishes, and before everyone celebrates you, I wanted a moment that belonged only to us.

Think of this as the first page of a love letter that has been waiting for you all along.

Open the letter 
If I Were There Right Now
❦

If I were there right now, I wouldn’t start with a speech.

I’d look at you for a few extra seconds, smile because you’re impossible not to smile at, and gently move that one strand of hair that always finds its way across your face.

I’d hold your hand, pull you a little closer, and whisper the first “Happy Birthday” so softly that only you could hear it.

I’d steal a hug that lasts longer than it should, and in that hug I’d try to say everything I never manage to say properly.

Until that moment comes, let these words be my arms around you.

Stay with me a little longer 
Five Reasons I Adore You
❦
The way you smile — It has this strange power to make ordinary moments feel unforgettable.
Your heart — You care deeply, even when you pretend you don’t.
The way you make me feel safe — Even from far away, you somehow make the world softer.
Your little habits — The tiny things you probably don’t notice are the things I remember the most.
You — Not because you’re perfect, but because you’re wonderfully, beautifully yourself.
There's something I want you to do 
Make a Wish
❦

Close your eyes for a second.

Forget the distance, forget the noise, and imagine that I’m standing right beside you.

Take a deep breath, think of one thing your heart truly wants, and let this moment belong only to you.

When you’re ready, blow out the candles.

Wish granted 
If we were together right now…
❦

If we were together right now, we’d probably be laughing over something completely stupid.

I’d insist on taking too many photos of you, and you’d pretend to be annoyed while secretly smiling in every single one.

We’d cut the cake, steal bites from each other’s plates, walk without any destination, and let the night become a memory we’d keep talking about for years.

I can’t give you that exact day today.

But I can promise you this: I’m already looking forward to the day when “if we were together” becomes “remember when we were.”

Come a little closer ❤️
Hold My Hand
❦

For the next five seconds, imagine this isn’t a screen.

Imagine it’s my hand reaching out to you.

No matter how many kilometres are between us, I want you to know that I’m still choosing you, still thinking of you, and still walking toward the future I hope we share.

So hold my hand for a moment…

and let the distance lose for five second

🤍

Press and hold.

Until I Can Tell You This in Person
❦

There are things that deserve more than text.

More than calls.

More than late-night messages.

This is one of them.

Until I can stand in front of you, look into your eyes, hold your face, and tell you how much you mean to me…

let this be my small promise that my love is already on its way to you.

Open the final letter ❤️
One Last Thing...

If you’ve reached this page, then you’ve walked through every little corner of the world I made for you.

I don’t know what made you smile, what made you pause, or what made your heart feel full, but I hope somewhere between these pages you felt what I was trying so hard to say.

You are one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

You have given me memories I replay without trying, comfort I never expected, and a kind of happiness that quietly changed me.

I know I’m not beside you today, and that hurts more than I can explain.

But distance is only measuring space, it has never been able to measure love.

So here’s my last wish for your birthday:

May you always feel loved, protected, understood, and cherished.

May every version of your future be kinder than your past.

And may I be lucky enough to keep celebrating birthdays with you for a very, very long time.

Happy Birthday, my love. Until I can say all of this in person… keep a little piece of my heart with you.

— Yours, Hritvik ❤️
